# Samsung — Galaxy/One UI Emergency Alert Usability Feedback

**Subject:** Galaxy public safety: regional emergency alert clarity and accessible community readiness

Dear Samsung Galaxy / One UI Team,

Following the October 9, 2026 Panama earthquake, we respectfully request consideration of clearer on-device information about emergency alert availability for travelers and residents.

**Specific product feedback:**
- Clearly identify Cell Broadcast / government emergency alerts support by region, operator and roaming status in Galaxy Settings, without implying universal coverage.
- Improve accessible readiness guidance, using tactile, spoken and multilingual alerts and simple public-source explanations.
- Where officially authorized, prioritize reliable emergency alert delivery via the operating system and carrier; do not depend solely on consumer push notifications or social networks.
- Provide a documented path for vetted humanitarian partners to integrate appropriately with Android notifications and official local alerts, under strict source verification and privacy constraints.

Our independent MIT-licensed research project, *Alerta Solidaria*, includes a Galaxy-style community map, localized help requests, donation listings, and optional WhatsApp sharing. It is a pilot, not a certified early warning or rescue system, and is not affiliated with Samsung.

We respect Samsung's policy against unsolicited ideas via Developer Support. We do not request that support accept a source archive. This message is intended for the product-feedback channel in Samsung Members or any formally invited partnership program.

Respectfully,
Independent open-source contributors to Alerta Solidaria
