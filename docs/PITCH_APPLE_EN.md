# Apple — iPhone Public Safety Alerts: Specific Platform Feedback and Open Source Reference

**Subject:** iPhone public safety improvement: more consistent regional earthquake and emergency alert access

Dear Apple iPhone / iOS Public Safety Team,

The earthquake in Panama on October 9, 2026 highlighted a usability gap: travelers may not know whether government alerts work in the country they are visiting, and third-party applications cannot offer equivalent guarantees. We would appreciate Apple reviewing the discoverability and regional coverage information for emergency notifications on iPhone.

**Specific feedback on existing iPhone/iOS functionality:**
- Provide a clear, readable status screen showing whether local government emergency alerts are supported by country/carrier, with accessible explanations and a test notification route where authorities permit.
- Clarify for travelers whether on-device emergency alerts will work with roaming and what settings and limitations apply.
- Improve documented integration pathways for vetted public-safety providers, with human-reviewed entitlement access for critical notifications.
- Expand accessible multilingual on-device safety guidance, including earthquake protective actions and verified official emergency resources.

A separate MIT-licensed community research package, *Alerta Solidaria*, demonstrates opt-in community mapping, supplies requests and WhatsApp sharing. It is an **experimental prototype**—not an early-warning system and not an emergency dispatch service. We are not asking Apple to ingest unsolicited code or to commit to integration.

If there is a suitable invitation-based technical partnership process for emergency preparedness or public sector collaborations, we would welcome guidance on the appropriate route. Otherwise, please treat this solely as iPhone feature feedback, consistent with Apple's submission policy.

Respectfully,
Independent open-source contributors to Alerta Solidaria

**Submission guidance:** Feedback Assistant for specific API enhancement requests, or Apple's iPhone product feedback form for existing feature usability. Do not attach source code without an accepted channel.
